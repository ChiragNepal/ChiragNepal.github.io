# Chirag Nepal, Ph.D.

**Assistant Professor, Loma Linda University**  
_Computational Genomics | Epigenetics | Precision Oncology_

---

### 🔬 About Me  
Computational genomicist specializing in clinical genomics, epigenetics, and precision oncology. Experienced in leveraging large-scale (epi)genetic and clinical data for hypothesis generation and translational research, with a focus on identifying biomarkers, drug resistance mechanisms, and immuno-oncology applications.

Strong expertise in bioinformatics, high-throughput data analysis, and machine learning applied to cancer research. Proven team leader with a strong track record of high-impact publications.

---

### 🧬 Current Research Interests
- Epigenetic burden and chromosomal instability in tumors  
- Methylation and transcriptional remodeling in cancer progression  
- Single-cell and spatial omics approaches  
- Computational methods for multi-omics integration  
- Immune regulation and tumor plasticity in the context of epigenomic reprogramming
